🧪 Projects

Provincial Innovation Project 2025.10 – 2026.04

Research on Drug Discovery Based on Multimodal Self-Supervised Learning

Undergraduate Innovation and Entrepreneurship Training Program.

This project focuses on drug-target interaction prediction under challenges such as scarce labeled data, insufficient representation, and limited interaction modeling. It adopts a self-supervised learning strategy, integrates multimodal data during training, and introduces a cross-attention mechanism to capture drug-target relationships for high-accuracy association prediction.

National Second Prize 2025.09

A Study on Smoke Screen Interference Bomb Deployment Strategy Based on Three-Layer Solution Architecture of Network Search, Fine Search and Differential Evolution（基于网络搜索、精细搜索和差分进化三层求解架构的烟幕干扰弹投放策略研究）

National College Student Mathematical Contest in Modeling (CUMCM).

📄 click to download the related paper

Modern warfare commonly uses drones to deploy interference bombs, forming smoke screens in specific airspace to disrupt enemy missiles. From an optimization perspective, this paper establishes a model to optimize the effective smoke screen duration against missiles, solves it using methods such as the vector angle method, and provides optimal deployment strategies for smoke screen interference bombs under different defense scenarios. Our project won the National Second Prize.
